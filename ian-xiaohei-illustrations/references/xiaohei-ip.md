# Xiaohei IP

## Character definition

Xiaohei is the fixed visual IP of Ian's inline article illustrations.

By default, Xiaohei appears in every image. Xiaohei is not a mascot, not a sticker, not a cute decoration — it is an absurd worker seriously participating in the operation of the system.

## Appearance

- A small solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- The body can be a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole, or a dark block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular outline with a hand-drawn feel.
- Expression: blank, dazed, calm, serious.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Deadpan humor; never acts cute.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- Seems to genuinely own a piece of the work inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Common duties

Make Xiaohei carry the core action:

- Hauling raw material.
- Pulling cables that converge information sources.
- Stuck inside a breakpoint.
- Operating the "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a filtering funnel.
- Cutting open the "material fish".
- Stamping handoff scripts.
- Leading the handoff path by the hand.
- Holding a warning sign while peering into a pit.
- Reaching out of a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Nearby: moving bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, taking notes.

## Forbidden

- Don't draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Don't draw it as a children's cartoon character.
- No elaborate outfits, emoji faces, or sparkly eyes for Xiaohei.
- Don't let Xiaohei just stand in a corner watching.
- Don't let Xiaohei steal the structural expression.
- Don't draw Xiaohei too commercial, too rounded, or too polished.

## Judgment criterion

If the image's core metaphor still fully holds after removing Xiaohei, then Xiaohei is too decorative; rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the action subject.
